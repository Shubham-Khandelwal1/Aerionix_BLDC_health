Aerionix silkscreen artwork - 26 October 2026

The PCB contains a monochrome vector rendition of the supplied Aerionix logo
and three original quadcopter icons, all on F.SilkS.

Logo: 16 mm wide, centered at approximately (114.500, 107.683) mm.
Drone centers and nominal sizes:
  (110.900, 110.200) mm - 2.8 mm
  (114.200, 119.300) mm - 3.8 mm
  (114.400, 123.500) mm - 3.4 mm

The artwork uses native silkscreen polygons, circles and lines, grouped in the
PCB Editor as Aerionix logo and Drone 1/2/3. It adds no electrical footprints.
The gold raster artwork was reduced to a single ink color, with transparent
and white background artifacts omitted. The original source is retained here.

All pre-existing board records were verified unchanged before saving. No
copper, component positions, reference text, design rules or zones changed.
Native DRC: zero violations and zero unconnected items. The four existing
board-only mounting-hole schematic-parity notices remain.
