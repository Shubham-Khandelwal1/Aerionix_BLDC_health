These are material limitations from the earlier review, not open PCB connections after this pass.

A. Schematic ERC
Current ERC is 167 findings: 23 errors and 144 warnings. Existing issues include unused MCU pins without explicit no-connect markers, regulator power-driving declarations, off-grid connections and pin-type/library consistency warnings. Resolve each against the actual circuit and firmware intentions. Do not blanket-suppress ERC or add no-connect markers to pins that firmware may need.

B. Four mechanical parity notices
The PCB contains MH2, MH5, MH6 and MH7 without corresponding schematic symbols. These are mechanical mounting-hole entries, not missing signal connections. Add matching mechanical schematic symbols in a separate documentation cleanup if strict schematic/PCB reference parity is required. Do not delete the mounting holes to clear these notices.

C. USB differential verification
The USB copper was deliberately retained in this pass. Earlier review did not establish a controlled 90-ohm differential impedance or matched path lengths. Fixed 0.20 mm traces on the retained two-layer stackup cannot be certified to an impedance target without the fabricator's dielectric/copper data and a field calculation. Confirm connector mechanics, pair return-path coverage, ESD provisions and firmware pull-up behavior before fabrication.

D. Crystal and regulator checks
Y1 remains the specified 8 MHz, CL20 pF crystal and its existing load-capacitor values remain unchanged. The local fanout change does not establish correct effective load capacitance, startup margin or oscillator EMI performance. Verify these against the actual purchased crystal and STM32 oscillator requirements. Regulator input/output capacitor placement improvements from the previous work remain; verify the purchased AMS1117 variant's required capacitance and ESR independently.

E. I2C and physical manufacturing
The earlier recommended starting I2C speed remains 100 kHz; no firmware settings were changed. Verify rise time and actual bus capacitance with the retained pull-ups. Confirm the selected manufacturer's drill, annular-ring, solder-mask and assembly capabilities, including retained legacy small vias and dense IMU pads. PCB DRC passing the project rules does not replace this manufacturer check.
